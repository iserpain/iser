Field notes / 001

Many paths.
One human question.

Across continents and centuries, people have asked how to live well, what happens when we die, and whether the universe holds a deeper order. Religious traditions offer distinct answers—not one secret code, but richly varied worlds of thought, practice, and community.

Explore these traditions on their own terms, with curiosity about both what they share and what makes each one singular.
